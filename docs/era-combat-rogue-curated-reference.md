# Era Combat Rogue curated recommendations

PrePull supports level-60 Era Combat Rogue recommendations for Pre-Raid and Phase 1. Combat is the historically accurate Classic talent-tree name; the dataset does not relabel it as the later “Outlaw” specialization.

Item identity, equipment slot, and acquisition records were independently reviewed against Warcraft Wiki’s Classic item and instance pages, including the [Nightslayer Armor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightslayer_Armor) and [Molten Core](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Molten_Core) records. Community gear discussions informed the set of candidates, but no third-party list was copied wholesale.

The authored priority is non-numeric. Main-hand and off-hand roles, sword/mace/dagger conditions, paired rings and trinkets, faction quest paths, and realistic versus aspirational acquisition are explicit data. The release makes no DPS, stat-weight, drop-rate, or farming-time claim.
