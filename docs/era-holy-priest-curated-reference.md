# Era Holy Priest curated recommendations

PrePull supports level-60 Era Holy Priest recommendations for Pre-Raid and Phase 1.

Item identity, equipment slot, and acquisition records were independently reviewed against Warcraft Wiki’s Classic item and instance pages, including [Vestments of Prophecy](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Vestments_of_Prophecy), [Molten Core](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Molten_Core), and the [Royal Seal of Eldre’Thalas Priest reward](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Royal_Seal_of_Eldre%27Thalas_(Priest)). Healing-oriented community references informed candidate discovery, but no guide was copied wholesale.

The authored priorities consider healing utility and mana sustain without converting them into a universal formula. One-hand plus off-hand, two-handed staff, wand, paired-ring, and paired-trinket legality use the shared evaluator. No healing-per-second simulation, stat weights, drop rates, or farming-time estimates are claimed.
