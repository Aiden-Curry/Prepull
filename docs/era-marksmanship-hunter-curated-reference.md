# Era Marksmanship Hunter curated recommendations

PrePull supports level-60 Era Marksmanship Hunter recommendations for Pre-Raid and Phase 1.

Item identity, equipment slot, and acquisition records were independently reviewed against Warcraft Wiki’s Classic item and instance pages, including [Giantstalker Armor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Giantstalker_Armor), [Striker’s Mark](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Striker%27s_Mark), and the [Royal Seal of Eldre’Thalas Hunter quest reward](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Royal_Seal_of_Eldre%27Thalas_(Hunter)). Community guides informed candidate discovery, but PrePull’s priorities and acquisition classifications were authored independently.

Ranged weapons are explicitly separate from one-hand or two-hand melee stat sticks. Ammunition and quiver optimization are not modeled in this release. Priorities are non-numeric and make no ranged-DPS, stat-weight, drop-rate, or farming-time claim.
