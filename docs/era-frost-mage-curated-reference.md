# Era Frost Mage curated recommendations

PrePull supports level-60 Era Frost Mage recommendations for Pre-Raid and Phase 1. The versioned datasets are `era-frost-mage-pre-raid-v1` and `era-frost-mage-phase-1-v1`.

The source CSVs record item identity, slot, acquisition activity, phase, faction or profession requirements, authored tier, notes, and provenance. Item identity and acquisition paths were reviewed against Blizzard Classic item metadata and Warcraft Wiki's Classic item and instance records. Where a shared raid item did not have a safely verified boss attribution, the dataset records the instance without inventing a boss.

Priority is an authored progression signal. It groups useful opportunities by activity and distinguishes realistic non-raid paths from aspirational or raid targets. It is not a simulated DPS score, a stat-weight calculation, a drop-rate estimate, or a farming-time promise. Spell power, hit, crit, intellect, and mana context can inform editorial review, but this release does not assign numeric weights to them.

The evaluator enforces paired ring and trinket choices, unique-item conflicts, faction quest paths, one-hand plus off-hand combinations, and two-handed weapon occupancy. Fire, Arcane, later Era phases, TBC, auction-house pricing, and live combat-log analysis are outside this dataset's scope.
